How this is worked out

  • Start: today's bank & cash estimate{% if current %} ({{ result.start | eur }}){% endif %}, the latest balance check plus everything logged since.
  • Repeats exactly as set up on Repeats, on their day of the month.
  • Income, business income included, as on the same days of that month a year earlier, so seasons (rentals, the bike season) carry over. A month with no history at all uses the average month.
  • Living costs as the average month of the last {{ result.history_months }} full month{{ 's' if result.history_months != 1 }}, spread over the month the way they usually fall. The average rather than the typical (median) month the other pages show: over several months the odd holiday or car repair does happen.
  • Not projected: business costs, which mostly don't come back the same way (last year's new bikes aren't this year's; add regular ones as repeats and expected purchases as planned one-offs), project and savings entries (one-offs like the house or a wedding; add what you expect as planned one-offs), entries outside our accounts, and past entries matching a repeat (same category, amount and direction), so a repeat isn't counted twice.
